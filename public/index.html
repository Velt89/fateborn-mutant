Brew

One free Fateborn per Agent ID, forever. Selling it does not give you a new brew — total stays 2,126.

Feed / Mutate

Every day, each Fateborn claims one free ingredient — Ember, Root, Bone, Crystal or Essence. Brew it into a Mutagen, feed it, and roll a mutation: stat boost, new trait, rarity up, or Corruption.

My ingredients / Mutagens appear in My Fateborn once the contract is live. Feeding burns the Mutagen; the NFT’s image, traits and stats update in its metadata.

Duel

Free, 3 duels a day per Fateborn. Winner mints a Gene Shard. Power decides the odds — 50 + the power gap, clamped to 10–90%.

Duels are single-transaction on-chain fights (no accept step in Season 1). The loser loses nothing but pride — the shard is minted to the winner.

Evolve / Ascend

Burn Gene Shards worth 5 (an Ancestor shard counts as 2) for one Fated evolution attempt. Only 100 Fated will ever exist, numbered #1–100.

A Fated Fateborn can Ascend: it becomes Eternal, earns 0.5% of the reward pool per week, locks a guaranteed Ancestor spot in Season 2 (its Season 2 Ancestor carries one of its traits), and can never be sold or transferred again. Irreversible.
